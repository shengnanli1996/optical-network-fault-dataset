# Responsible use

This dataset contains measurements from a controlled laboratory testbed and does not contain personal or participant data.

The dataset is intended for research, education, reproducible benchmarking, and the development or evaluation of optical-network fault-management methods. Users should:

- cite the dataset and its associated paper;
- preserve the stated units, topology boundaries, fault order, and experimental labels;
- report transformations, exclusions, and train/test splits when publishing results;
- avoid presenting laboratory performance as evidence of safe deployment in a production network without additional validation; and
- avoid attempting to reconstruct private management identifiers or credentials.

Hardware and product names describe experimental provenance only and do not imply vendor endorsement. The data are provided without a guarantee that a model trained on them is suitable for operational or safety-critical decisions.
